YouTube Clipper

Cut an exact piece out of any YouTube video and save it as a video or audio file. Free, no account, and everything runs on your own computer — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Download

Pick your computer, unzip, open the app. That's the whole install.

Download for Windows Windows 10 or 11 Download for Mac (Apple Silicon) Macs with an M1, M2, M3 or M4 chip — most Macs since 2021 Download for Mac (Intel) Older Macs. Check via Apple menu, About This Mac

First launch

The app isn't code-signed yet, so both systems ask once for permission:

  1. Windows: open YouTube Clipper.exe. On the blue "Windows protected your PC" screen click More info, then Run anyway. Only the first time.
  2. Mac: open YouTube Clipper. When macOS refuses, go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down and choose Open Anyway. Only the first time.

Your browser then opens the clipper page by itself.

How it works

  1. Paste a YouTube link and press Load. The video plays right on the page.
  2. Press Set start here and Set end here at the right moments, or type exact times. Fine-tune in steps of a second or a tenth.
  3. Preview your selection, pick video, original-quality audio, or MP3, and press Download. The file lands in your Downloads folder.

There's also a lossless mode that keeps YouTube's original video quality untouched, and a phone mode: turn on "Use from your phone" in the app and scan the QR code to use the clipper from a phone on the same WiFi.

The whole app works with keyboard only and with screen readers (NVDA, VoiceOver).
