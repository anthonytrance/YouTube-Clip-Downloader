YouTube Clipper

Cut an exact piece out of any YouTube video and save it as a video or audio file. Free, no account, and everything runs on your own computer — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Download

Pick your computer, unzip, open the app. That's the whole install.

First launch

The app isn't code-signed yet, so both systems ask once for permission:

  1. Windows: open YouTube Clipper.exe. On the blue "Windows protected your PC" screen click More info, then Run anyway. Only the first time.
  2. Mac: open YouTube Clipper. When macOS refuses, go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down and choose Open Anyway. Only the first time.

Your browser then opens the clipper page by itself.

How it works

  1. Paste a YouTube link and press Load. The video plays right on the page.
  2. Press Set start here and Set end here at the right moments, or use the keyboard shortcuts I for start and O for end. Type exact times or fine-tune in steps of a second or a tenth.
  3. Preview your selection, pick video, original-quality audio, or MP3, and press Download. The file lands in your Downloads folder.

There's also a lossless mode that keeps YouTube's original video quality untouched.

Use it from your phone

Your phone and computer must be on the same trusted home WiFi. In the clipper, choose Turn on phone access, then scan the QR code or open the address it shows.

Windows firewall

The first time you turn on phone access, Windows may ask whether to allow YouTube Clipper to communicate through the firewall. Select Private networks, clear Public networks, then choose Allow access.

If you previously blocked or dismissed that question, open Windows Security, then Firewall & network protection, Allow an app through firewall, and Change settings. Allow YouTube Clipper on Private networks only. If it is missing, choose Allow another app and select YouTube Clipper.exe from the unzipped app folder.

If it still fails after you previously chose Cancel or Don't allow, open Firewall & network protection, Advanced settings, then Inbound Rules. Remove only blocking rules named YouTube Clipper, restart the app, turn phone access on again, and allow it on the Private network.

Mac firewall

If macOS asks whether YouTube Clipper should accept incoming network connections, choose Allow. If you previously chose Deny, open Apple menu, System Settings, Network, Firewall, then Options. Add YouTube Clipper from the unzipped app folder if necessary and set it to Allow incoming connections.

Do not turn the firewall itself off. Allowing the app is safer than opening a permanent port. Phone access stops listening when you turn it off in the clipper or close the app.

The whole app works with keyboard only and with screen readers (NVDA, VoiceOver).
