## Mac

1. Download the file called YouTube-Clipper-mac-arm64.zip (for a MacBook
   from 2021 or newer) or YouTube-Clipper-mac-intel.zip (for older Intel
   Macs). If unsure which Mac it is: Apple menu, About This Mac. If it
   says Apple M1, M2, M3 or M4, take the arm64 one.
2. Double-click the zip to unpack it. You get a folder called
   "YouTube Clipper". You can move it anywhere, for example into
   Applications or just keep it in Downloads.
3. Open the folder and double-click "YouTube Clipper".
4. The first time, macOS will refuse and say the app is from an
   unidentified developer. This is expected, the app is not registered
   with Apple. To allow it: open System Settings, go to Privacy &
   Security, scroll down, and next to the message about YouTube Clipper
   click "Open Anyway", then confirm. You only ever do this once.
5. Your browser opens the clipper page by itself. If it does not, open
   your browser and go to: http://127.0.0.1:8574
6. Using it: paste a YouTube link, press Load. The video appears with a
   player. Play it, and at the moment your clip should begin press
   "Set start here". At the end press "Set end here". Fine-tune with the
   plus and minus buttons if needed, press "Preview clip" to check, pick
   a format (video or MP3), press "Download clip". The finished file
   lands in your normal Downloads folder.
7. To quit: close the small black window that opened with the app.

## Windows

1. Download YouTube-Clipper-windows.zip and unpack it (right-click,
   Extract All).
2. Open the folder and double-click "YouTube Clipper.exe".
3. The first time, Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC"
   screen. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". Only needed once.
4. From there everything works exactly like the Mac steps 5 to 7 above.

## Good to know

- Everything runs on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- A 15 second clip is typically ready in well under a minute.
- If a download ever fails with a strange error, close the app, open it
  again and retry. YouTube changes things sometimes and the app updates
  its own downloader in the background.
